# engine/validation Delta Specification

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every Modeled Scenario Variable Has An Evidence Class

Each clinical state variable used for display, event triggering, objective evaluation, tutor logic,
or debrief attribution SHALL be classified as directly sourced, calibrated to a sourced range,
derived by a published model, sourced discrete transition, authored teaching convention, or unknown/
unsupported. Unknown/unsupported variables SHALL NOT drive clinical teaching claims.

#### Scenario: A display value cannot outrun validation

- **WHEN** a new scenario displays or evaluates a variable
- **THEN** its evidence class, source/model, applicable population, units, precision, calibration
  target, and known failure modes exist in the validation manifest

#### Scenario: A teaching convention stays visible

- **WHEN** an authored convention drives timing, device response, state transition, or objective
- **THEN** validation tests the implementation against the declared convention and learner-facing
  provenance never describes it as patient-validated behavior

### Requirement: Scenario Validation Uses Independent Anchors

Golden traces SHALL detect regression but SHALL NOT be the only evidence of plausibility. Every new
modeled scenario family SHALL include at least one independent published benchmark, guideline-
consistent directional test, or qualified face-validity procedure that was not generated by the same
implementation.

#### Scenario: A self-consistent wrong model is caught

- **WHEN** a golden trace remains stable but violates an external range, ordering, conservation law,
  or direction of effect
- **THEN** the independent validation gate fails and identifies the external anchor

#### Scenario: No numerical benchmark exists

- **WHEN** a scenario uses a sourced discrete transition or qualitative syndrome with no defensible
  individual trajectory benchmark
- **THEN** the validation record limits claims to ordering/direction/state, defines a face-validity
  procedure, and forbids invented numeric accuracy

### Requirement: Sensitivity And Ablation Explain Complex Scenarios

Scenarios with more than one modeled cause SHALL test each contributor independently and in
combination so that debrief attribution, tutor observations, and recovery logic do not rely on a
single opaque composite.

#### Scenario: Each contributor matters detectably

- **WHEN** a complex scenario's contributor is ablated while all else remains fixed
- **THEN** at least one declared state, observation, or attribution changes in the predicted direction
  and the remaining course stays within bounds

#### Scenario: One contributor cannot dominate accidentally

- **WHEN** sensitivity sweeps span each declared calibration range
- **THEN** the primary teaching cues and objective reachability persist or the scenario narrows its
  envelope and records excluded combinations
