# engine/waveform-synthesis Specification

## Purpose

Specifies how the waveforms are actually generated. Every other capability assumes a realistic electrocardiogram, arterial pressure trace, capnogram, and plethysmogram exist; this one says where they come from. Waveform realism is the largest single technical risk in the project and the thing a clinician judges the simulator by in the first five seconds, so the method is named, published, and testable rather than left to whoever writes the canvas code.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Electrocardiogram Uses A Published Dynamical Model

The electrocardiogram SHALL be generated by the three coupled ordinary differential equations of McSharry, Clifford, Tarassenko, and Smith (*IEEE Trans Biomed Eng* 2003;50:289–94, PMID 12669985), in which a trajectory circles a limit cycle in the (x, y) plane and is displaced in z by Gaussian terms placed at the P, Q, R, S, and T events. Morphology SHALL be controlled by the published per-event parameters (angle, amplitude, width), so a rhythm is a parameter set rather than a hand-drawn picture.

#### Scenario: The model is implemented from the paper, not copied from GPL code

- **WHEN** the implementation's provenance is reviewed
- **THEN** it derives from the published equations and parameter tables, contains no code copied from the PhysioNet ECGSYN reference implementation or any other GPL source, and the licensing note records this so the project's permissive license stays clean

#### Scenario: A normal sinus rhythm is recognizable to a clinician

- **WHEN** a clinician reviewer inspects the generated lead II trace at 25 mm/s
- **THEN** P wave, QRS complex, ST segment, and T wave are present with correct relative timing and amplitude, and the face-validity rubric records the rating

#### Scenario: Heart rate variability is present and physiological

- **WHEN** the trace runs at a steady mean heart rate in an awake patient
- **THEN** beat-to-beat interval varies with a respiratory component, and that variability is attenuated under general anesthesia, because a metronomic trace reads as fake

#### Scenario: Rate changes do not distort morphology incorrectly

- **WHEN** heart rate rises from 60 to 140 beats per minute
- **THEN** the QT interval shortens with rate in the published manner rather than the whole complex scaling uniformly, and the RR interval shortens as expected

### Requirement: Rhythm Library With Named Morphologies

The engine SHALL provide a library of rhythms, each a declared parameter set with its own generation rule: normal sinus, sinus bradycardia and tachycardia, atrial fibrillation, supraventricular tachycardia, first-degree and complete heart block, ventricular tachycardia, ventricular fibrillation, asystole, pulseless electrical activity, and paced rhythm. Each SHALL declare whether it produces a mechanical pulse.

#### Scenario: Electrical and mechanical activity are separate

- **WHEN** pulseless electrical activity is active
- **THEN** the electrocardiogram shows organized complexes while the arterial trace is flat and the plethysmogram is non-pulsatile, which is the entire diagnostic point of that rhythm

#### Scenario: Atrial fibrillation is irregularly irregular

- **WHEN** atrial fibrillation runs for 60 seconds
- **THEN** the RR intervals show no repeating pattern, P waves are absent, and an automated test confirms the interval distribution differs from sinus rhythm with variability

#### Scenario: A rhythm change is a transition, not a jump

- **WHEN** the rhythm changes from sinus to ventricular fibrillation
- **THEN** the transition occurs at a beat boundary with no discontinuity artifact in the trace, and the alarm and the numeric invalidation follow within one beat

### Requirement: Arterial Pressure Waveform

The arterial pressure trace SHALL be synthesized from the current systolic pressure, diastolic pressure, heart rate, and vascular state, showing an upstroke, a dicrotic notch, and a diastolic runoff, with the notch position and the waveform shape responding to systemic vascular resistance.

#### Scenario: The waveform reflects the underlying hemodynamics

- **WHEN** systemic vascular resistance falls during vasodilation
- **THEN** the upstroke steepens relative to the runoff and the dicrotic notch moves, so the shape itself carries information a learner can read

#### Scenario: Respiratory variation appears with hypovolemia

- **WHEN** the patient is hypovolemic and mechanically ventilated
- **THEN** systolic pressure varies with the ventilator cycle, the variation magnitude scales with the volume deficit, and pulse pressure variation is computable from the rendered trace

#### Scenario: Damping is a rendering state, not a pressure change

- **WHEN** the arterial line is damped by artifact
- **THEN** the waveform loses its dicrotic notch and high-frequency content and the displayed pressure narrows toward the mean, while the underlying state vector is untouched

### Requirement: Capnogram With Real Phase Structure

The capnogram SHALL be generated with its four named phases — inspiratory baseline, expiratory upstroke, alveolar plateau, and inspiratory downstroke — with the alpha angle between the upstroke and the plateau as an explicit, modulated parameter.

#### Scenario: Bronchospasm produces the shark-fin morphology

- **WHEN** bronchospasm is active
- **THEN** the expiratory upstroke slope decreases and the alveolar plateau slope increases, widening the alpha angle into the characteristic shark-fin shape, and the degree of change scales with obstruction severity

#### Scenario: Morphology changes before the number does

- **WHEN** bronchospasm begins
- **THEN** the waveform shape change is detectable in the rendered trace before the end-tidal value moves outside its alarm limits, teaching the learner to read the shape

#### Scenario: Other named capnogram patterns are available

- **WHEN** the capnogram pattern library is enumerated
- **THEN** it includes at minimum the normal rectangular trace, the shark fin of obstruction, the curare cleft of returning neuromuscular function, rebreathing with a raised baseline, cardiogenic oscillations, esophageal intubation with an absent or rapidly decaying trace, and circuit disconnection with loss of the waveform entirely

### Requirement: Plethysmogram Follows Perfusion

The pulse oximetry plethysmogram SHALL be generated from stroke volume, vascular tone, and the same cardiac cycle that drives the electrocardiogram and arterial trace, so the three remain phase-coherent.

#### Scenario: The three traces agree beat for beat

- **WHEN** all three traces render together
- **THEN** each arterial upstroke and each plethysmographic upstroke follows its QRS complex at a physiological delay, verified by an automated cross-correlation test

#### Scenario: Poor perfusion degrades the trace realistically

- **WHEN** peripheral perfusion falls from vasoconstriction or low cardiac output
- **THEN** the plethysmographic amplitude falls, the saturation reading becomes less reliable, and the interface indicates low signal quality rather than silently reporting a confident number

### Requirement: Rendering Is Decoupled From Generation

Waveform generation SHALL run in the solver worker at the simulation tick rate and emit a sample buffer; the canvas SHALL only draw from that buffer. The renderer SHALL never synthesize physiology.

#### Scenario: A dropped frame loses pixels, not signal

- **WHEN** the renderer misses frames under load
- **THEN** the sample buffer is unaffected, the trace resumes without a gap in the underlying signal, and the recorded transcript replays identically

#### Scenario: Sample rate is sufficient for the morphology

- **WHEN** the electrocardiogram is generated
- **THEN** it is sampled at no less than 250 Hz so that QRS morphology is preserved, with lower rates permitted for the slower capnogram and plethysmogram, and each rate is declared per signal

### Requirement: Waveform Realism Is Reviewed, Not Assumed

Waveform morphology SHALL be part of the expert face-validity review, with clinicians rating each rhythm and each pattern against what they would expect to see on a monitor.

#### Scenario: An unconvincing waveform blocks the claim, not the release

- **WHEN** a reviewer rates a morphology as unrealistic
- **THEN** either the parameters are corrected, or the pattern is removed from the library, or the limitation is recorded in the limitations register and the pattern is labeled as schematic in the interface

#### Scenario: Reference images are cited for each pattern

- **WHEN** a pattern is added to the library
- **THEN** its definition cites the published or textbook source describing that morphology, so a reviewer can compare rather than judge from memory
