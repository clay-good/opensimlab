# Organizational endorsement

An endorsement is a named organization's approval of a defined, clinically reviewed release for a
defined educational use. It is never “medical certification,” blanket approval, or permission for
clinical decision-making.

An endorsement record must name the legal and display names, public website, authorized signer and
role, authority statement, exact modules/scenarios and versions, learner population, practice
region, exclusions, review method, signed date, expiration within 24 months, conflicts, signature
artifact hash, and revocation state.

The public wording is “endorsed by `<organization>` for `<scope>`.” It must link to the record and
show expiration and exclusions. A record requires a `clinically_reviewed` base. Any covered clinical,
tutor, objective, limitation, or numeric change creates a new version and removes the endorsement
until renewed. Revocation or expiration remains visible in the historical record and removes the
active badge immediately.

No organization is currently recorded or endorsed.
