# Waveform provenance and licensing note

**Recorded at the time of writing, not retrofitted.**

## Electrocardiogram

`src/modules/anesthesia/waveforms/ecg.ts` implements the dynamical model published in:

> McSharry PE, Clifford GD, Tarassenko L, Smith LA. A dynamical model for generating
> synthetic electrocardiogram signals. *IEEE Trans Biomed Eng* 2003;50(3):289–94.
> PMID 12669985.

The implementation derives from the three coupled ordinary differential equations and
the parameter table printed in that paper.

**No code was read from, copied from, translated from, or adapted from the PhysioNet
ECGSYN reference implementation, or from any other GPL-licensed source.** ECGSYN is
distributed under the GNU General Public License; incorporating any of it would place a
copyleft obligation on this repository, which is MIT licensed. The equations and the
published parameter values are the mathematics of the paper, not its code, and are used
here on that basis.

### What is ours rather than the paper's

The paper does not print a rate-scaling table. The rate behavior in this file is Open Sim
Lab's own encoding of two relationships a clinician expects, documented inline on
`EcgEvent.rateExponent`:

- QRS duration is essentially rate independent, so the Q, R and S events carry exponent `0`.
- The QT interval scales with the square root of the RR interval, the Bazett relationship,
  so the P and T events carry exponent `0.5`.

Both are asserted by `tests/unit/waveform-ecg.test.ts`.

The rhythm library in `rhythms.ts` is likewise our own parameter sets over the published
event structure. Each entry names the source describing that morphology so a clinical
reviewer can compare against a reference rather than judge from memory.

## Arterial pressure, capnogram, plethysmogram

No published dynamical model equivalent to McSharry exists for these three signals, and
the project says so rather than implying one.

- **Arterial** is generated by a two-element Windkessel driven by a stroke-volume ejection
  pulse. The Windkessel is textbook cardiovascular physiology, not a specific paper's code.
- **Capnogram** is a piecewise construction of the four named clinical phases, with the
  alpha angle as the modulated parameter.
- **Plethysmogram** is a shaped pulse locked to the electrocardiogram's R peaks.

These three are the most likely items to fail the clinical face-validity review, which is
recorded as a known risk in `openspec/changes/mvp-anesthesia-alpha/design.md`.

## Pharmacology

Every model parameter is transcribed by hand from its primary publication into typed
TypeScript under `src/modules/anesthesia/pharmacology/models/`. There is no dependency on,
import of, or vendored copy of the sibling Hypnos project or any other pharmacology
dataset. `tests/arch/dependencies.test.ts` enforces this.
