# Clinical reviewer guide

A clinical review accepts a precise educational scope for one exact content version. It is not a
general endorsement of Open Sim Lab, proof of patient-level accuracy, or approval for clinical use.

## Review sequence

1. Confirm your declared specialty scope and competing interests in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
2. Read the evidence brief, primary/authoritative sources, authored defaults, hazards, limitations,
   and validation anchors.
3. Run the expert, common-error, recovery, no-action, unsafe/refused, regional, and boundary paths.
4. Check every learner-facing claim, tutor intervention, objective window, debrief attribution, and
   transfer-to-practice warning against the modeled behavior.
5. Record objections in `/content-review` and export the local notes file. A flag is not a signature.
6. Sign only the exact version and declared domains you are qualified to accept.

Ask for narrowing when evidence cannot support precision. Require another unconflicted reviewer
when a relevant financial relationship exists. Pediatric, obstetric, neonatal, and other specialist
content needs matching declared scope. A clinical or numerical change invalidates the prior review;
spelling-only preservation requires the future machine-checkable nonclinical-diff policy.

The editorial board is currently empty and all content remains unreviewed. See
[GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) for the authoritative status.
