# Acute pulmonary edema respiratory-support reassessment

## Intended rehearsal

Recognize progressive respiratory failure despite documented initial noninvasive support and
syndrome treatment; keep mentation, work, oxygenation, ventilation, acid-base status, pressure,
perfusion, congestion, alternatives, and precipitants together; activate airway-capable experienced
help; and hand off active failure without turning a browser control into treatment or a procedure.

## Evidence boundary

- The 2021 ESC heart-failure guideline recommends early noninvasive positive-pressure ventilation
  for acute pulmonary edema with respiratory distress and recommends intubation for progressive
  respiratory failure despite oxygen or noninvasive ventilation. It also requires pressure
  monitoring because positive pressure can reduce preload and blood pressure.
- The official ERS/ATS noninvasive-ventilation guideline supports CPAP or bilevel NIV for acute
  respiratory failure due to cardiogenic pulmonary edema. It does not make one respiratory rate,
  saturation, blood-gas value, or elapsed interval a universal failure rule.
- This lab therefore uses a fixed whole-patient failure pattern and records escalation only. It does
  not select an interface, mode, setting, oxygen concentration, drug, airway technique, or treatment.

## Authored vignette

A 69-year-old woman arrives with a fixed hypertensive pulmonary edema pattern: HR 118/min, RR
34/min, BP 196/118 mmHg, and pulse-coherent room-air SpO₂ 82%. An experienced team starts monitored
noninvasive support with titrated oxygen and delivers loop-diuretic and pressure-appropriate
vasodilator therapy. Thirty minutes later she is drowsy but rousable with shallow RR 12/min, HR
104/min, BP 108/68 mmHg, SpO₂ 86% in an authored nominal FiO₂ 0.60 condition, a fixed continuous
interface-sampling waveform with EtCO₂ 60 mmHg, pH 7.18, PaCO₂ 68 mmHg, and PaO₂ 58 mmHg. A pulse and preserved central perfusion remain present, congestion persists,
and the lower rate is authored fatigue rather than improvement. No later response is supplied.

## Exclusions

The screen does not examine; acquire or interpret monitoring, blood gas, ECG, imaging, or ultrasound
data; diagnose; operate or change oxygen or noninvasive support; select a device, interface, flow,
FiO₂, pressure, PEEP, mode, drug, dose, or treatment; intubate; perform an airway procedure;
determine disposition or prognosis; or predict response, resolution, or outcome.
