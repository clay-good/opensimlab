# Severe acidemia evidence brief

## Scope

A fixed adult ICU case with septic shock, moderate-to-severe AKI, high-anion-gap metabolic acidosis,
and superimposed respiratory acidosis. The lesson covers recognition, confirmation, mixed-disorder
analysis, preservation of safe ventilatory compensation, cause-directed care, individualized buffer
and kidney-support planning, and immediate reassessment. It does not diagnose an acid-base disorder,
set a ventilator, prescribe bicarbonate, or provide kidney support.

## Evidence translated into the lesson

- The 2026 Surviving Sepsis Campaign conditionally suggests bicarbonate for septic shock with severe
  metabolic acidemia (pH 7.2 or lower) and moderate-to-severe AKI, with very low certainty. It also
  conditionally suggests against bicarbonate solely to improve hemodynamics or reduce vasopressors
  in hypoperfusion-induced lactic acidemia.
- The French expert panel recommends confirming metabolic acidosis with blood-gas analysis, testing
  for mixed disorders, using albumin-corrected anion gap, treating the cause, and preserving
  ventilatory compensation without making normal pH the goal or inducing unsafe mechanics.
- BICARICU-2 enrolled adults with pH 7.20 or lower and moderate-to-severe AKI. Bicarbonate did not
  lower 90-day mortality; kidney-support use was lower among secondary outcomes. The lesson therefore
  neither promises survival benefit nor turns one pH into an automatic prescription.
- KDIGO preserves emergent kidney support for life-threatening acid-base imbalance and states that
  evidence does not support a standard pH or bicarbonate threshold for starting it.

## Boundaries and disputed practice

Sampling, acid-base calculation, airway and ventilator strategy, perfusion targets, source control,
buffer formulation and delivery, electrolyte treatment, toxicology care, and kidney-support timing
depend on the full patient trajectory and local expertise. The browser performs none of them. No
copyrighted algorithm, prescription, dosing table, or universal pH trigger is reproduced.
