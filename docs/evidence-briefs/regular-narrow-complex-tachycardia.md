# Evidence brief: regular narrow-complex tachycardia

## Intended rehearsal

This intermediate urgent-cardiology case rehearses distinguishing a stable regular narrow-complex
tachycardia from the unstable pathway, preserving mechanism uncertainty, recording vagal and
protocol-bounded adenosine intent, and reassessing the whole patient before recurrence follow-up.

## Evidence boundary

- The 2025 AHA tachyarrhythmia algorithm determines instability from hypotension, altered mental
  status, shock, ischemic discomfort, or acute heart failure, not heart rate alone.
- For a stable regular narrow-complex rhythm, the algorithm supports vagal maneuvers and consideration
  of adenosine with monitoring, access, and expert consultation. This lab omits dosing and delivery.
- The ACC/AHA/HRS SVT guideline emphasizes that regular SVT includes several possible mechanisms and
  that ongoing observation, medication, or ablation decisions depend on symptom burden, recurrence,
  patient preference, and individual context.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not examine; acquire or interpret an ECG or test; diagnose the rhythm mechanism;
perform a vagal maneuver; select, prescribe, dose, or deliver medication; perform cardioversion or
ablation; determine disposition; predict recurrence or prognosis; or assess psychomotor competence.
