# Evidence brief: torsades de pointes

## Intended rehearsal

This advanced monitored Cardiology lesson rehearses immediate unsynchronized-shock intent for
sustained pulsed polymorphic VT, followed by an authored post-team rhythm review and long-QT-specific
recurrence prevention and ownership.

## Evidence boundary

- All sustained polymorphic VT is electrically unstable and requires immediate unsynchronized shock;
  magnesium, QT review, synchronization, or an energy calculation must not delay it.
- Torsades is polymorphic VT associated with known or suspected prolonged QT outside the episode.
  The fixed pre-event report supplies QTc; the learner does not calculate QT during polymorphic VT.
- Magnesium may be considered to suppress recurrent polymorphic VT associated with long QT. Routine
  magnesium is not recommended for polymorphic VT with a normal QT.
- Correct electrolyte abnormalities and review QT-prolonging drivers. Expert rate support for
  bradycardia- or pause-dependent recurrence is individualized and not performed in this lab.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not examine; acquire or independently interpret pulse, ECG, monitoring, laboratory,
or imaging data; diagnose inherited or acquired cause; select shock energy or sedation; operate a
defibrillator; deliver shock, CPR, oxygen, magnesium, electrolyte, medication, infusion, pacing, or
isoproterenol; assess capture; choose a device; determine disposition or prognosis; or predict
recurrence, benefit, or outcome.
