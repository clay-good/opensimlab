# Evidence brief: ARDS lung-protective ventilation

## Intended rehearsal

This intermediate adult ICU case rehearses translating height and sex into predicted body weight,
reducing an injurious tidal-volume and plateau-pressure pattern, reassessing mechanics, gas exchange,
synchrony, and circulation together, and escalating persistent moderate-severe hypoxemia through a
protocolized PEEP/FiO₂ and experienced prone-team plan.

## Evidence boundary

- Official ATS/ESICM/SCCM guidance recommends tidal volumes of 4–8 mL/kg predicted body weight and
  plateau pressure below 30 cm H₂O; 6 mL/kg predicted body weight is the authored starting intent.
- The 2024 ATS update continues lung-protective ventilation and recommends prone positioning for
  more than 12 hours daily in severe ARDS. Higher PEEP without prolonged recruitment maneuvers is a
  conditional option in moderate-severe ARDS and requires patient-specific monitoring.
- Gas exchange, pH, carbon dioxide, synchrony, airway pressures, and hemodynamics require serial
  review. Permissive hypercapnia is a monitored consequence, not a universal target or permission to
  ignore contraindications.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not diagnose ARDS, sample blood, calculate a real-patient prescription, program a
ventilator, measure plateau pressure, select PEEP or FiO₂, manage an airway, sedation, paralysis,
fluids, or vasopressors, perform proning, recruitment, imaging, or ECMO, or assess respiratory-therapy,
procedural, liberation, disposition, prognosis, or outcome competence. All findings and responses are
fixed authored teaching panels.
