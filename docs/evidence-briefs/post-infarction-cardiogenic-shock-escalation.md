# Evidence brief: post-infarction cardiogenic-shock escalation

## Intended rehearsal

This advanced cardiac-ICU case begins after reported culprit-vessel PCI and initial vasoactive
support at a hospital without advanced shock capability. It rehearses detecting failure to improve
despite verified initial support, reopening causes, activating a shock team and regional-center consultation,
recording a bounded transport bridge, and handing off unresolved work after elapsed reassessment.

## Evidence boundary

- ACC's 2025 guidance defines cardiogenic shock by sustained tissue hypoperfusion irrespective of
  blood pressure and emphasizes serial, multi-organ reassessment.
- The same guidance treats shock-team activation and possible transfer to a higher level of care as
  part of triage, with support shaped by phenotype, trajectory, candidacy, risk, and local resources.
- A post-PCI report does not close recurrent ischemia, mechanical, right-heart, rhythm, bleeding,
  vasodilated, or obstructive contributors when perfusion worsens.
- Temporary mechanical support is selective, not routine. This lab supplies no device, threshold,
  agent, dose, or universal target.
- The Critical Care cardiogenic-shock lab starts before revascularization and prioritizes initial
  support plus culprit PCI. This Cardiology lab starts after reported PCI and verified initial support;
  failure to improve triggers reopened-cause review, regional consultation, and an unresolved-work handoff.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not examine; acquire or interpret monitoring, laboratory, ECG, echo, angiography,
or hemodynamic data; diagnose; prescribe or deliver drugs or fluid; select or place a device;
perform PCI or surgery; transport; determine disposition or prognosis; or predict outcome.
