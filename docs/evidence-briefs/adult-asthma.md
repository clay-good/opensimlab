# Adult asthma exacerbation evidence brief

## What this slice teaches

One fixed adult emergency-department vignette asks the learner to assess severity from speech,
work of breathing, oxygenation, and expiratory flow while checking immediate alternative causes.
The learner records controlled oxygen, a conservative fixed inhaled bronchodilator bundle, early
systemic-corticosteroid intent, and whole-patient reassessment before automatic repeat treatment.

## Current source floor

The May 2026 GINA strategy report adds standardized acute-care flowcharts for adults, adolescents,
and children 6–11 years. It lowers the supplemental-oxygen threshold to saturation below 92%, sets
95% as the adult upper target when oxygen is used, deliberately reduces SABA doses because
overtreatment and lactic acidosis can mimic worsening asthma, includes pMDI-and-spacer technique,
and emphasizes response review after the initial bronchodilator. It also supports early systemic
corticosteroids in all but the mildest acute-care presentations, where possible within 1 hour.

## Authored vignette and modeled behavior

- A fixed severe presentation supplies words-only speech, accessory-muscle use, widespread
  expiratory wheeze, room-air SpO₂ 89%, and peak expiratory flow 32% predicted.
- One sustained obstruction event drives the shared shark-fin capnogram and gas-exchange model.
- Controlled oxygen changes inspired oxygen to a bounded teaching setting. A fixed 6-puff
  salbutamol plus 4-puff ipratropium pMDI-and-spacer action reduces modeled obstruction.
- Systemic corticosteroid is intent only because its delayed clinical effect is outside the short
  simulation. Reassessment reveals a fixed repeat peak flow of 55% predicted and reads canonical
  symptoms, signs, oxygenation, and waveforms.

## Deliberate exclusions

This is not a diagnostic system, examination or spirometry trainer, inhaler-technique trainer,
oxygen calculator, drug calculator, prescription, or complete acute-asthma pathway. It does not
model blood gas, imaging, device formulation, preparation, lung delivery, individualized or repeat
dosing, SABA toxicity, magnesium, non-invasive or invasive ventilation, intensive care, admission
or discharge, controller selection, adherence or technique review, triggers, written action plans,
local protocol, recurrence, or outcome.
