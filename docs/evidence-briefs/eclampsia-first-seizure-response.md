# Evidence brief: eclampsia first-seizure response

## Decision this lesson rehearses

Recognize a supplied eclampsia pattern after a first generalized convulsion in late pregnancy and
activate qualified maternal stabilization, recurrent-seizure, severe-hypertension, airway-ready,
obstetric and fetal response immediately. Review neurologic and other dangerous seizure causes in
parallel, then preserve recurrence, aspiration, stroke, organ, fetal and delivery risk through a
fixed later report and handoff. This is distinct from the no-seizure postpartum warning-sign lab
and the Anesthesia lab's bounded prophylaxis and pressure treatment before an already-decided birth.

## Explicit exclusions

oxygen; ventilate; establish access; measure glucose or pressure; examine; assess reflexes, clonus,
