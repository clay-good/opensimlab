# Oxytocin-associated uterine tachysystole evidence brief

Status: development draft, not clinically reviewed. Checked August 26, 2026.

## Fixture boundary

The authored case supplies oxytocin augmentation, 6 contractions per 10 minutes averaged over 30 minutes, a worsening qualified fetal-heart report, stable normoxic maternal observations, absent selected danger reports, a qualified corrective response, and a fixed 6-minute early-recovery report. The learner recognizes and coordinates only. No examination, palpation, fetal-monitor interpretation, infusion operation, position, oxygen, fluid, drug, dose, procedure, birth decision, disposition, or outcome is controlled.

## Evidence carried into the lab

- ACOG defines uterine tachysystole as more than 5 contractions in 10 minutes averaged over 30 minutes and says to report whether fetal-heart decelerations are associated. The definition applies to spontaneous and stimulated labour.
- NICE advises continuous CTG with oxytocin, reducing or stopping oxytocin when contractions exceed the intended frequency, discontinuing it immediately for a pathological CTG, and obtaining urgent senior review.
- Fetal monitoring is not a standalone diagnostic tool. Maternal observations, contraction frequency, labour progress, evolving fetal-heart baseline, variability and decelerations, antenatal and intrapartum risk, and the woman's preferences belong in one assessment.
- Cause-specific conservative measures include avoiding the supine position, treating hypotension when present, and reducing excessive contraction frequency by reducing or stopping oxytocin; tocolysis or expedited birth may require qualified case-specific consideration.
- Routine maternal facial oxygen is not recommended for fetal-heart concerns in a normoxic patient. IV fluid should not be used reflexively for fetal-heart abnormalities without maternal hypotension or sepsis.

## Deliberate nonclaims

The infusion history, maternal and fetal reports, and fixed 6-minute course are fictional. Reduced contraction frequency and short-term fetal-heart improvement do not prove a treatment effect, durable fetal safety, cause, oxytocin-restart eligibility, labour or birth plan, newborn safety, disposition, prognosis, or outcome. This cognitive lab does not teach CTG interpretation, oxytocin operation, tocolysis, birth technique, or replace current local protocols, direct care, or multidisciplinary simulation.
