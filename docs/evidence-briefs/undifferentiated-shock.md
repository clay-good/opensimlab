# Undifferentiated shock evidence brief

## What this slice teaches

One fixed adult emergency-department vignette asks the learner to recognize shock from serial
tissue-perfusion evidence, inspect more than arterial pressure, obtain a fixed lactate result,
assess fluid responsiveness and cardiac phenotype, give one bounded targeted crystalloid
challenge, and reassess the same perfusion markers before choosing further escalation.

## Evidence boundary

- The 2025 ESICM shock guideline emphasizes serial tissue-perfusion assessment, capillary refill,
  dynamic predictors of fluid responsiveness, targeted rather than liberal fluid, cardiac-output
  or stroke-volume monitoring in nonresponders, and echocardiography as first-line imaging to
  assess shock type.
- The guideline does not support diagnosing or managing shock from one number. Its scope is adult
  critically ill patients; this educational ED vignette uses the same initial assessment concepts
  without claiming to reproduce an ICU monitoring pathway.
- The 2024 ESICM fluid-choice guideline conditionally favors crystalloids over albumin and balanced
  crystalloids over isotonic saline for volume expansion in critically ill adults, with low or
  very-low certainty for several comparisons.

## Authored vignette and modeled behavior

- The fictional patient starts with tachycardia, hypotension, delayed capillary refill, cool skin,
  altered attention, low urine output, and a fixed elevated lactate.
- A fixed focused cardiac-ultrasound result shows low filling with preserved left- and
  right-ventricular systolic appearance and no pericardial effusion. The control records review of
  authored findings; it does not simulate image acquisition or interpretation skill.
- A fixed passive-leg-raise teaching maneuver reveals an authored positive measured stroke-volume
  estimate. It is not the canonical monitor state and does not estimate individual fluid responsiveness.
- One bounded 500 mL balanced-crystalloid challenge uses the existing shared fluid and circulation
  capability, retaining 125 mL in its teaching model. Authored serial perfusion reassessment
  determines whether the learner closes the loop; no individual pressure or stroke-volume response
  is promised. Further liberal fluid and etiologic treatment remain outside this first slice.

## Deliberate exclusions

This is not a diagnostic tool, hemodynamic calculator, ultrasound trainer, or resuscitation
protocol. It does not accept real-patient data or model examination technique, invasive monitoring,
laboratory sampling, infection, hemorrhage source, myocardial ischemia, pulmonary embolism,
tension pneumothorax, tamponade, pregnancy, pediatric physiology, vasopressor choice or dose,
antimicrobials, blood products, source control, procedures, disposition, or individual outcome.
The fixed pattern must not be generalized to every patient with shock.
