# Evidence brief: cardiogenic shock

## Intended rehearsal

This advanced adult ICU case rehearses recognizing an acute-MI cardiogenic-shock trajectory,
activating multidisciplinary help, integrating a left-sided congested phenotype, recording a
bounded perfusion bridge without primary fluid loading, prioritizing cause control, and reassessing.

## Evidence boundary

- ACC's 2025 guidance emphasizes early recognition, serial severity and phenotype assessment,
  multidisciplinary shock systems, cause-directed care, and escalation shaped by trajectory.
- The 2025 ACS guideline retains prompt revascularization as central treatment for acute-MI shock.
- ESICM recommends echocardiography as first-line imaging for shock type and dynamic, serial
  perfusion and hemodynamic assessment when initial therapy is insufficient.
- ESICM does not recommend fluid resuscitation as primary treatment for left-sided cardiogenic
  shock. This congested case therefore records no blind fluid challenge.
- Norepinephrine is a bounded bridge intent here, not a universal pressure target, dose, access,
  delivery method, or claim that perfusion has been restored. Inotrope and temporary-support choices
  remain patient-, phenotype-, trajectory-, risk-, resource-, and expert-dependent.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not examine, acquire or interpret monitoring, ECG, laboratory, or echo data,
diagnose shock, deliver oxygen or medication, select access or dose, perform catheterization or
revascularization, choose or place mechanical support, transfer, determine disposition, predict
outcome, or assess cardiovascular, critical-care, imaging, procedural, or device competence.
