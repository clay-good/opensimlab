# COPD exacerbation evidence brief

## What this slice teaches

One fixed adult emergency-department vignette asks the learner to assess an acute COPD worsening,
check common respiratory and cardiac mimics, interpret one authored blood gas, and identify the
features that determine immediate support. The learner records controlled oxygen, initial inhaled
bronchodilators, short-course systemic-corticosteroid intent, an antibiotic indication based on
purulent sputum, and reassessment for treatment response or ventilatory escalation.

## Current source floor

GOLD 2026 classifies a suspected exacerbation from dyspnea, respiratory rate, heart rate, room-air
oxygen saturation, inflammatory evidence, and blood gas when obtained. It recommends an inhaled
short-acting beta2-agonist with or without a short-acting anticholinergic as initial
bronchodilation, prefers air-driven rather than oxygen-driven nebulization when a nebulizer is
used, recommends 40 mg prednisone-equivalent daily for 5 days for a significant exacerbation, and
supports antibiotics when sputum is purulent, a prior sputum culture is positive, or mechanical
ventilation is required. Oxygen is titrated and blood gases are reviewed for carbon-dioxide
retention; ventilatory support is escalated when respiratory failure does not respond.

## Authored vignette and modeled behavior

- A fixed moderate presentation supplies increased dyspnea, respiratory rate 28/min, heart rate
  104/min, room-air SpO2 90%, increased purulent sputum, and a blood gas of pH 7.36, PaCO2 52 mmHg,
  and PaO2 58 mmHg. Fixed statements exclude focal consolidation, edema, pneumothorax, and abrupt
  pulmonary-embolism features without proving the diagnosis.
- One sustained obstruction event drives the shared expiratory waveform and bounded gas-exchange
  model. Authored monitor anchors keep the respiratory rate and oxygen saturation consistent with
  the fixed ED findings.
- Controlled oxygen targets 88-92%. A fixed air-driven short-acting beta2-agonist plus
  anticholinergic intent reduces modeled obstruction; corticosteroid and antibiotic choices remain
  intent-only because formulation, delivery, delayed pharmacology, microbiology, and prescribing
  are outside this short simulation.
- Reassessment reveals a fixed pH 7.38 and PaCO2 48 mmHg with improved work of breathing and asks
  whether noninvasive ventilatory escalation is currently indicated.

## Deliberate exclusions

This is not a diagnostic system, examination trainer, blood-gas analyzer, oxygen calculator,
nebulizer or inhaler technique trainer, drug calculator, prescription, or complete COPD pathway.
It does not model chest imaging, ECG, viral testing, cultures, eosinophils, CRP, sputum sampling,
formulation, individualized or repeat dosing, bronchodilator toxicity, antibiotic selection,
allergy or resistance, noninvasive-ventilation setup, invasive ventilation, admission, discharge,
smoking cessation, maintenance therapy, pulmonary rehabilitation, local protocol, recurrence, or
outcome.
