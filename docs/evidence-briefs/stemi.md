# STEMI evidence brief

## Source floor

- The 2025 multisociety ACS guideline recommends an initial oral aspirin loading dose for ACS, recommends dual antiplatelet therapy, and retains rapid invasive reperfusion as the organizing treatment for STEMI.
- The 2021 chest-pain guideline states that a 12-lead ECG should be acquired and interpreted within 10 minutes of arrival because it identifies patients needing urgent coronary reperfusion.
- Primary PCI is preferred for eligible STEMI when it can be delivered promptly. Routine oxygen is not recommended when SpO2 is at least 90%; supplemental oxygen is reserved for confirmed hypoxia below 90%.

## Authored vignette

The fictional patient has 45 minutes of ongoing central pressure with diaphoresis and nausea, a fixed 12-lead ECG showing anterior ST elevation with reciprocal inferior depression, BP 146/92 mmHg, SpO2 95%, and no authored shock, pulmonary edema, aortic-dissection pattern, or recent phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor exposure. The facility is declared PCI-capable. The learner can activate the STEMI pathway, record primary-PCI intent, record an aspirin load and dose-free P2Y12/anticoagulation intents, then reassess before handoff.

## Exclusions

The lab does not acquire or teach 12-lead ECG interpretation, troponin testing, echocardiography, or angiography. It does not diagnose a real patient; calculate ischemic or bleeding risk; select a P2Y12 inhibitor or anticoagulant; adjudicate all contraindications; teach nitrate, opioid, fibrinolytic, PCI, access, or stent technique; model infarct size, reperfusion, arrhythmia, shock, mechanical complication, transfer, disposition, secondary prevention, or outcome.
