# Evidence brief: endotracheal-tube migration after repositioning

## Intended rehearsal

This intermediate adult ICU case rehearses reassessment after patient movement, integrating
pre/post tube-depth documentation with bilateral ventilation, airway pressure, exhaled volume,
capnography, oxygenation, and the whole patient before recording bounded experienced-airway
correction intent and proving the response.

## Evidence boundary

- Adult head and neck movement can move a tracheal-tube tip unpredictably toward or away from the
  carina. The case therefore makes the repositioning event and pre/post evidence explicit.
- Continuous exhaled carbon dioxide supports ongoing tracheal ventilation but does not establish
  correct depth or bilateral lung ventilation.
- Unilateral observations, changed depth, higher airway pressure, lower exhaled volume, and
  progressive gas-exchange change converge in this authored state. Mucus plugging, pneumothorax,
  circuit or ventilator problems, atelectasis, consolidation, and other causes remain open until
  appropriate real assessment.
- The 22 cm and 25 cm markings are fictional case facts, not universal insertion targets.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not turn or examine a patient; auscultate; inspect securement, cuff, tube, circuit,
or ventilator; measure depth or pressure; deliver oxygen or ventilation; reposition, withdraw,
advance, exchange, or secure a tube; acquire or interpret imaging; perform bronchoscopy; diagnose;
determine disposition; or predict outcome. All observations and responses are authored proxies.
