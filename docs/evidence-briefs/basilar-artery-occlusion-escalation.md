## Source synthesis

The 2026 AHA/ASA guideline gives a Class 1, Level A recommendation for EVT within 24 hours in
patients with basilar occlusion, baseline mRS 0–1, NIHSS at least 10, and pc-ASPECTS at least 6. This
lab supplies each fact, records escalation instead of eligibility adjudication, and predicts no
individual benefit.

ATTENTION randomized 340 selected patients with basilar occlusion within 12 hours in China. Good
functional status occurred more often with EVT, with symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage and
procedural complications also reported. BAOCHE randomized 217 selected patients presenting 6–24
hours after basilar-occlusion symptoms in China and found a favorable functional-outcome shift with
procedural and hemorrhage risks; it stopped early for superiority. Selected populations, China
settings, protocols, and outcome distributions limit universalization.
