# High neuraxial block obstetric coordination evidence brief

Status: development draft, not clinically reviewed. Checked August 26, 2026.

## Fixture boundary

The authored case supplies a recent qualified epidural top-up, rapid ascending sensory and motor findings, arm weakness, difficulty breathing, weak speech, bradycardia, hypotension, fetal bradycardia, and a fixed qualified-support report. Qualified staff have already stopped injection, called for help, begun uterine displacement, and stayed close. The learner recognizes and coordinates only; no examination, block assessment, monitoring interpretation, airway care, oxygen, ventilation, position, fluid, vasopressor, drug, dose, anesthesia, birth, newborn assessment, disposition, or outcome is controlled.

## Evidence carried into the lab

- The OAA obstetric QRH describes potentially rapid progression after neuraxial local anesthetic from hypotension, bradycardia and breathing difficulty through arm paralysis, impaired consciousness, apnea and unconsciousness. It couples early help and reassurance with qualified airway, ventilation, circulation, uterine-displacement, monitoring, fetal and birth work, while keeping vasovagal, aortocaval, local-anesthetic-toxicity, embolic and concealed-hemorrhage alternatives open.
- The 2024 OAA intrathecal-catheter guideline identifies agitation, hypotension, bradycardia, upper-limb weakness, dyspnea and difficulty speaking as warning features and emphasizes monitoring, communication, labeling and rapid respiratory and circulatory support.

## Deliberate nonclaims

The injection, examination, maternal and fetal values, and fixed 4-minute report are fictional. Partial physiologic improvement does not prove a treatment effect, block recession, airway safety, fetal recovery, awareness prevention, completed birth, newborn safety, disposition, or outcome. This cognitive lab is intentionally separate from the existing modeled high-spinal treatment scenario and is not clinical, airway, medication, obstetric, or procedural competence.
