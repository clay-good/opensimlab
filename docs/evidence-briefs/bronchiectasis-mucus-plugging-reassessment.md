# Bronchiectasis mucus plugging with focal collapse

## Intended rehearsal

This intermediate Respiratory Medicine lab rehearses recognizing a meaningful change in secretion-
clearance capacity, integrating fixed focal imaging without making it diagnostic proof, recording
experienced and individualized airway-clearance intent, reviewing a partial response, and handing
off persistent focal collapse and unresolved cause.

## Evidence boundary

- The 2025 ERS adult-bronchiectasis guideline strongly recommends teaching airway-clearance
  techniques. It says they are best taught by an experienced respiratory physiotherapist, that no
  one technique is proven superior, and that treatment should be personalized.
- The 2023 ERS statement supports airway clearance as part of usual adult bronchiectasis care but
  describes limited comparative evidence and no established optimal frequency or session count.
- CT-visible mucus plugging can support considering airway clearance in selected people. Imaging
  still does not prove one cause or exclude an occult obstruction.
- The BTS guideline supports physiotherapy review when a patient deteriorates and consideration of
  bronchoscopy for localized disease when an obstructing lesion or foreign body remains possible.
  This lab therefore connects experienced evaluation for persistent focal collapse; it does not
  make bronchoscopy routine.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not examine, test cough, collect or assess sputum, acquire or interpret imaging or
microbiology, diagnose, select or deliver an airway-clearance technique, oxygen, suction, drug, or
treatment, perform bronchoscopy or another procedure, determine disposition or prognosis, or
predict resolution, recurrence, durable benefit, or outcome. All findings and responses are fixed
authored reports.
