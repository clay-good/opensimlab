## Evidence application

NICE NG234 defines cord-compression neurological signs and symptoms in a person with cancer as an
oncologic emergency requiring immediate coordination. It recommends urgent whole-spine MRI as soon
as possible and within 24 hours, early qualified corticosteroid care when neurological signs are
present, individualized immobilization or movement decisions, and definitive intervention timed to
the neurological trajectory. NICE quality statement 3 reinforces the whole-spine MRI standard.
The lab applies those system priorities without turning them into learner-selected prescriptions.
