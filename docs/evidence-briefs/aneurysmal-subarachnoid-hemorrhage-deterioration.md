## Source synthesis

The 2023 AHA/ASA aSAH guideline describes DCI as an important, potentially preventable complication
and supports frequent neurological assessment plus multimodal monitoring when examination is
limited. CTA, CT perfusion, transcranial Doppler, and EEG can contribute in context; no single fixed
test substitutes for the changing patient. The guidance supports urgent qualified evaluation and
management of symptomatic vasospasm or DCI. It does not support prophylactic hemodynamic augmentation
or hypervolemia, and this scenario does not turn any management recommendation into a learner control.

The Vergouwen consensus separates clinical DCI and cerebral-infarction outcome definitions from
angiographic vasospasm. Its research definition uses a neurological change lasting at least one hour
after reasonable alternatives are excluded, but that standardizes research outcomes; it is not a
reason to delay urgent clinical escalation when a new deficit has been present for 35 minutes.
