# Evidence brief: maternal sepsis with postpartum deterioration

## Rehearsal value

This lab practices recognition of infection plus organ dysfunction without fever, score, or source
closure; immediate multidisciplinary ownership; parallel infectious and noninfectious review; bounded
qualified-team sepsis intent; strict reassessment; and active-risk handoff. It is not a screening,
antimicrobial, fluid, vasopressor, source-control, or newborn-care trainer.

## Source floor

1. Society for Maternal-Fetal Medicine. *Consult Series #67: Maternal sepsis*. 2023, reaffirmed 2025
   and endorsed by ACOG. This supports emergency recognition, avoiding one screening tool, cultures
   without substantial antimicrobial delay, lactate, broad-spectrum therapy ideally within 1 hour,
   response-guided balanced crystalloid, vasopressor escalation, and urgent source control.
2. World Health Organization. *Statement on maternal sepsis*. 2017. This supports maternal sepsis as
   life-threatening organ dysfunction from infection during pregnancy, childbirth, post-abortion,
   or the postpartum period and preserves maternal and newborn risk.

Consulted on August 26, 2026. Local sepsis, antimicrobial, resuscitation, source-control,
critical-care, infection-prevention, and newborn policy remains controlling.

## Authored defaults

- The fictional patient is 29 years old and 38 hours after an unplanned cesarean birth following
  24 hours of ruptured membranes.
- Qualified staff supply temperature 39.1°C, HR 132/min, BP 88/52 mmHg (MAP 64), RR 28/min,
  pulse-coherent room-air SpO2 96%, slowed but oriented responses, reduced urine output, uterine
  tenderness, malodorous lochia, WBC 19.4 × 10^9/L, creatinine 1.4 mg/dL from 0.7, and lactate
  4.2 mmol/L. Learner actions do not create these findings.
- A strict 30-minute qualified-team report after bounded intent supplies HR 122/min, BP 94/58 mmHg
  (MAP 70), RR 24/min, SpO2 98% on supplied support, temperature 39.0°C, clearer responses, urine
  output and repeat lactate still pending, and source control unresolved.
- All findings and timing are authored and deterministic.

## Hazard and scope boundaries

- Do not require fever, one score, one value, or confirmed microbiology; do not let a uterine pattern
  close wound, urinary, respiratory, abdominal, thromboembolic, hemorrhagic, hypertensive,
  medication, or other causes.
- Do not expose tests, an antimicrobial, dose, route, access, fluid volume, vasopressor, pressure
  target, oxygen setting, imaging, evacuation, drainage, surgery, or disposition as learner controls.
- Do not claim modest improvement proves antimicrobial effect, adequate resuscitation, source
  control, organ recovery, safe transfer, or maternal/newborn outcome.
- Preserve dignity, pain, privacy, feeding/newborn support, VTE risk, survivor support,
  antimicrobial review, source control, and local-protocol ownership.

## Verification contract

The expert path is six ordered actions with elapsed gates before reassessment and handoff. Tests cover
no-action, order and time refusal, hostile text, generic drug/fluid/vasopressor shortcuts, adjacent
adult-sepsis isolation, deterministic state, exact authored claims, route/SEO/report coverage, and
the absence of learner examination, scoring, diagnosis, treatment, procedure, disposition, or
outcome claims.
