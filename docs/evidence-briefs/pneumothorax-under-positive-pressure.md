# Evidence brief: pneumothorax under positive pressure

## Decision this lesson rehearses

When airway pressure rises as oxygenation and circulation deteriorate during positive-pressure
ventilation, check bilateral ventilation, escalate, give 100% oxygen, act immediately on severe
tension physiology, and reassess. The lesson records decompression intent but does not teach a
procedure.

## Source basis

1. Association of Anaesthetists. *Quick Reference Handbook: Guidelines for crises in anaesthesia*.
   June 2023 edition. Consulted August 24, 2026. Sections 2-2, 2-3, and 2-4 support 100% oxygen,
   checking chest symmetry and breath sounds, considering pneumothorax with increased airway
   pressure, and recognizing pneumothorax or high intrathoracic pressure as a cause of hypotension.
2. Resuscitation Council UK. *Special circumstances guidelines*. Published October 27, 2025.
   Consulted August 24, 2026. The tension-pneumothorax section supports clinical examination or
   point-of-care ultrasound for diagnosis during hemodynamic instability and immediate chest
   decompression when tension pneumothorax is suspected with severe hypotension.
3. European Resuscitation Council. *Guidelines 2025: Special Circumstances in Resuscitation*.
   Resuscitation 2025;215(Suppl 1):110753. Consulted August 24, 2026. This is the primary guideline
   referenced by the current Resuscitation Council UK special-circumstances guidance.

## Implemented teaching bounds

- At 60 simulated seconds, one authored left-pleural event rises on a 4-second teaching time
  constant. The monitor trajectory lowers stroke volume, cardiac output, pressure, end-tidal carbon
  dioxide, and saturation. It does not calculate pleural gas volume or pressure.
- A bilateral-ventilation assessment returns markedly reduced left chest movement and air entry,
  preserved right air entry, and unchanged documented tracheal-tube depth. This is a scripted
  finding, not a physical-examination model or diagnostic proof.
- Accepted left-chest decompression intent stops the active drive. Residual effects clear on a
  12-second teaching time constant so the learner can observe reassessment rather than an
  instantaneous reset.
- Decompression credit comes only from an accepted engine event. A raw request, unsupported
  action, pre-event action, or duplicate action cannot earn credit.

## Explicit exclusions

The browser does not model airway pressure, lung compliance, pleural pressure, gas volume,
barotrauma, lung injury, airway resistance, tube migration, auscultation technique, ultrasound,
radiography, differential diagnosis, cardiac arrest, later chest drainage, recurrence, procedural
site selection, needle or thoracostomy technique, equipment choice, procedural complications, local
protocol, surgery, postoperative care, or team performance. The values are deterministic teaching
outputs, not diagnostic thresholds or individual predictions.
