# Evidence brief: pediatric sepsis without shock

## Learning contract

This lab rehearses recognition of a supplied suspected-infection and organ-dysfunction pattern,
distinction from current shock, experienced-care activation, source and organ review, time-aware
reassessment, and active-risk handoff. It is not a sepsis screen, Phoenix calculator, diagnosis,
test, antimicrobial, fluid, oxygen, procedure, treatment, or disposition algorithm.

## Evidence anchors

- The 2026 Surviving Sepsis Campaign pediatric guideline defines pediatric sepsis as infection with
  life-threatening respiratory, cardiovascular, coagulation, or neurological organ dysfunction.
  For probable sepsis without shock it supports a time-limited rapid evaluation and, when concern
  remains substantiated, timely qualified antimicrobial therapy. Cultures should precede therapy
  only when they do not substantially delay it. Most recommendations relevant here have low or
  very-low certainty, so the authored minute-25 treatment is a case fact, not a universal clock.
- The 2024 international consensus identifies pediatric sepsis as suspected infection with a
  Phoenix Sepsis Score of at least 2 and septic shock as sepsis with at least 1 cardiovascular
  point. Platelets below 100,000 per microliter and INR above 1.3 each contribute 1 coagulation
  point, with that subscore capped at 2. Phoenix is a classification after organ dysfunction is
  evident, not an early screening tool or a substitute for local recognition pathways.
- Serial whole-child assessment remains essential. Preserved blood pressure, a lower temperature,
  or a falling lactate does not erase persistent organ dysfunction, prove source control, establish
  durable recovery, or determine disposition.

## Authored fixture

The child, suspected infection, coagulation results, supplied Phoenix classification, qualified
care record, current no-shock state, and strictly later partial change are fixed. The healthy-child
engine profile is source-composed scaffolding, not a validated sepsis or treatment model. No
treatment effect, source, pathogen, durable recovery, prognosis, disposition, or outcome is proven.
