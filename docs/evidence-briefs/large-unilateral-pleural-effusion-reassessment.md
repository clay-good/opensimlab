# Large unilateral pleural effusion reassessment

## Intended rehearsal

Reconcile a symptomatic large unilateral effusion with current breathing and perfusion; record
experienced, image-guided diagnostic and slow symptom-relief aspiration intent; stop at an authored
symptom checkpoint without chasing a volume; review a fixed paired-fluid classification while
keeping causes open; connect every pending result to a qualified owner; and hand off unresolved
pleural work.

## Evidence boundary

- The 2023 BTS pleural-disease guideline recommends a systematic approach to an undiagnosed
  unilateral effusion. Imaging, clinical context, and pleural fluid inform one another; absent
  malignant features or negative cytology cannot independently close the cause pathway.
- The 2023 BTS pleural-procedures statement requires thoracic ultrasound for effusion aspiration
  and recommends slow manual or gravity drainage. Chest tightness, pain, persistent cough, worsening
  breathlessness, or concerning oxygenation change should prompt stopping before any general volume
  ceiling becomes a target.
- This lab records experienced-team intent and reviews fixed reports. It does not teach a procedural
  site, device, technique, rate, volume, sample calculation, or etiologic diagnosis.

## Authored vignette

A 63-year-old woman has 6 weeks of progressive dyspnea, dry cough, and left chest heaviness. She is
alert with HR 104/min, RR 26/min, BP 128/76 mmHg, room-air SpO₂ 91%, and warm perfusion. Fixed
radiograph and thoracic-ultrasound reports show a large predominantly free-flowing left effusion,
compressive change, and an experienced-operator access window without pneumothorax or marked shift.
After qualified intent and elapsed time, an experienced team reports 850 mL removed slowly before
persistent cough and mild chest tightness prompt stopping. Symptoms, rate, oxygenation, and lung
expansion improve, while residual effusion and cause remain open. A fixed paired sample is reported
as exudative; cytology, microbiology, and selected cause-directed studies remain pending.

## Exclusions

The screen does not examine; acquire or interpret monitoring, imaging, ultrasound, laboratory,
cytology, microbiology, or pleural-fluid data; calculate criteria; diagnose; choose a site, needle,
catheter, drain, suction, rate, volume, drug, oxygen device, biopsy, bronchoscopy, indwelling
catheter, pleurodesis, thoracoscopy, surgery, systemic therapy, or treatment; aspirate or perform a
procedure; determine disposition or prognosis; or predict recurrence, durable benefit, or outcome.
