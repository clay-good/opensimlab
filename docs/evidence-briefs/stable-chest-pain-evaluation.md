# Stable chest-pain evaluation

## Intended rehearsal

Distinguish a fixed stable exertional symptom pattern from an acute or changing presentation,
characterize symptom and functional burden, estimate obstructive-coronary-disease likelihood from
the whole clinical context, record a patient-specific noninvasive-testing pathway, and preserve an
explicit safety net without announcing a diagnosis.

## Evidence boundary

- The 2021 AHA/ACC chest-pain guideline recommends structured risk assessment for stable chest
  pain, discourages the descriptor “atypical,” targets diagnostic testing to people most likely to
  benefit, and emphasizes shared decision-making about benefits, risks, costs, radiation, and
  alternatives.
- The 2024 ESC chronic-coronary-syndrome guideline recommends a risk-factor-weighted clinical
  likelihood model and selection of testing from likelihood, patient characteristics, and local
  expertise. Very-low-likelihood patients may not need testing; this authored case is explicitly
  not placed in that tier.
- The lab does not calculate or reproduce a proprietary or region-specific score and does not
  select one universal test. Its history, risk factors, ECG report, likelihood label, and plan are
  fixed teaching facts.

## Exclusions

The screen does not examine, acquire or interpret an ECG, calculate risk, measure exercise
capacity, order or perform calcium scoring, CCTA, stress testing, angiography, or another test;
diagnose coronary disease or ischemia; prescribe medication; determine disposition; or predict
events or outcome.
