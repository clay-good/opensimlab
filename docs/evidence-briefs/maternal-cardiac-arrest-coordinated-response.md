# Evidence brief: maternal cardiac arrest coordinated response

## Decision this lesson rehearses

Activate a prepared pregnancy cardiac-arrest response and arrest clock immediately, connect the
supplied later-pregnancy context, prepare qualified resuscitative delivery at the arrest location
from recognition, and coordinate parallel maternal, delivery, newborn, hemorrhage and support work.
The lesson is distinct from the pulse-present suspected-amniotic-fluid-embolism pattern and from the
generic first-cycle PEA lab.

## Source basis

1. American Heart Association. *2025 Guidelines for CPR and ECC: Adult and Pediatric Special
   Circumstances of Resuscitation*, section 19.3. Consulted August 26, 2026. This supports standard
   resuscitation plus continuous manual left lateral uterine displacement when fundal height is at
   or above the umbilicus, early airway priority, upper-extremity access, removal of fetal monitors,
   preparation for resuscitative delivery from arrest recognition, completion by minute 5 when
   circulation has not returned, delivery at the arrest location, and resource-dependent ECPR.
2. American Heart Association. *Cardiac Arrest in Pregnancy Algorithm*. 2025. Consulted August 26,
   2026. This provides the low-clutter team sequence and parallel delivery-readiness boundary.
3. Alliance for Innovation on Maternal Health. *Cardiac Conditions in Obstetric Care Patient Safety
   Bundle*. 2025. Consulted August 26, 2026. This supports multidisciplinary readiness, respectful
   care, communication, systems learning, and patient- and support-network-centered care.

## Implemented teaching bounds

- A 32-year-old at 32 weeks 4 days has a witnessed collapse 30 seconds before the learner surface.
  Qualified staff supply unresponsiveness, absent normal breathing, absent central pulse, organized
  electrical activity at 48/min without mechanical circulation, and a fundal height above the
  umbilicus. Qualified standard chest compressions have already begun.
- No preceding major hemorrhage, seizure, fever, rash, anesthetic exposure, trauma, known cardiac
  disease, IV magnesium infusion, or confirmed cause is authored.
- The first learner action activates the prepared pregnancy-arrest team and clock. Pregnancy-
  specific responsibilities, delivery readiness and reversible causes are then coordinated in
  parallel; the learner performs no physical or treatment action.
- The fixed minute-4 report preserves pulseless electrical activity and states only that qualified
  resuscitative delivery is beginning at the arrest location while advanced life support continues.
  The lesson stops before delivery completion; maternal circulation, fetal or newborn condition,
  cause, treatment effect, hemorrhage and outcomes remain open. The minute-5 goal is a team-readiness
  target, not a biologic futility threshold.

## Explicit exclusions

The browser does not assess responsiveness, breathing, a pulse, fundal height, bleeding or fetal
status; examine; acquire or interpret monitoring, ECG, capnography, ultrasound, laboratory or
another test; diagnose or exclude a cause; perform or score CPR; select or deliver uterine
displacement, oxygen, ventilation, airway care, access, fluid, blood, drug, dose, route, rate,
target, defibrillation, pacing, ECMO, resuscitative delivery, surgery or another procedure; operate
fetal monitoring; transfer the patient; terminate resuscitation; determine disposition or
prognosis; or report maternal or newborn outcome.
