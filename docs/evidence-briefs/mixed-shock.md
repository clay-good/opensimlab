# Evidence brief: mixed shock

## Intended rehearsal

This advanced ICU case rehearses recognizing discordant cardiac and vasodilatory shock clues,
integrating a fixed hemodynamic panel in treatment context, supporting tone and output without blind
fluid loading, keeping parallel causes open, and reassessing the trajectory.

## Evidence boundary

- The 2024 mixed-cardiogenic-shock framework describes low output, elevated filling pressure, and
  inappropriately low vascular resistance as a pattern that should prompt mixed-shock consideration.
  It explicitly treats proposed ranges as context-dependent rather than strict diagnostic cutoffs.
- ACC 2025 and ESICM 2025 emphasize repeated severity, phenotype, perfusion, echo, and hemodynamic
  assessment, particularly when shock does not respond to initial therapy.
- The authored pneumonia pathway uses the 2026 sepsis guideline's immediate-resuscitation,
  vasopressor, source-control, and frequent-reassessment principles without recreating prescribing.
- The case records tone support and expert review of output support. It does not claim one universal
  agent, target, inotrope, fluid plan, catheter threshold, or temporary-support strategy.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not examine, acquire or interpret monitoring, catheter, ECG, laboratory, echo, or
imaging data, calculate hemodynamics, diagnose, deliver oxygen, fluid, or drugs, select access or
dose, revascularize, treat infection, perform source control, place mechanical support, transfer,
determine disposition, predict outcome, or assess cardiac, critical-care, catheter, imaging,
prescribing, procedural, or device competence.
