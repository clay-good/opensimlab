# Bilevel NIV selection in acute COPD

## Intended rehearsal

This intermediate Respiratory Medicine lab rehearses selecting a closely monitored bilevel NIV
trial after verified initial COPD care has not resolved acute-on-chronic acidotic hypercapnic
respiratory failure, then using elapsed whole-patient and blood-gas reassessment to preserve failure
guards and airway-capable rescue readiness. It is a support-selection lesson, not device training.

## Evidence boundary

- The 2026 ATS guideline strongly recommends NIV for acute hypercapnic respiratory failure to reduce
  mortality and invasive mechanical ventilation. Its conditional HFNC option is limited to selected
  less-severe hypercapnia with mild acidemia, for example pH above 7.25, when close monitoring and
  prompt escalation to NIV are available. The authored pH of 7.28 is not used as an isolated cutoff.
- The 2017 ERS/ATS guideline strongly recommends bilevel NIV for COPD with acute or acute-on-chronic
  respiratory acidosis. A lower pH increases failure risk but does not create an absolute lower
  threshold; close monitoring and rapid access to invasive ventilation remain essential.
- Early improvement in pH, respiratory rate, work, comfort, mentation, and ventilation during the
  first several hours supports continuation, but no single change proves durable success. The fixed
  first-hour panel is therefore partial evidence, not an outcome.
- CPAP provides continuous distending pressure but not the same inspiratory pressure assistance.
  HFNC can support oxygenation and comfort, but it is not the selected first support in this authored
  acidotic hypercapnic COPD pattern.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not examine; acquire or interpret blood gas, imaging, oximetry, capnography, or
another test; diagnose; select or deliver oxygen, medication, sedation, suction, mask ventilation,
intubation, or treatment; choose a device, interface, fit, pressure, PEEP, backup rate, trigger,
cycle, flow, FiO₂, or oxygen target; operate a ventilator; perform a procedure; decide a ceiling of
care; determine weaning, disposition, or prognosis; or predict durable success or outcome. Initial
care, qualified NIV delivery, and both physiologic panels are fixed authored reports.
