# Failed obstetric intubation: oxygenation first

## Evidence boundary

This fixed rehearsal follows the OAA/DAS obstetric failed-tracheal-intubation algorithm and open-access guideline. After 2 unsuccessful attempts, the qualified team declares failed intubation, calls experienced help, and prioritizes oxygenation. A second-generation supraglottic airway is the authored effective rescue device. Adequate oxygenation is distinguished from can’t-intubate-can’t-oxygenate, while deterioration readiness remains active.

The wake-or-proceed review is deliberately individualized. Maternal and fetal urgency, staff experience, surgical complexity, aspiration risk, alternative anesthesia, the airway device, ventilation, and airway hazards are all relevant. No single factor or learner control decides the course.

## Fixed case

Two failed qualified-team intubation attempts are followed by declared failure and effective second-generation supraglottic ventilation. The fixed 3-minute report supplies stable oxygenation and capnography, persistent fetal bradycardia, no supplied airway hazard, and a qualified multidisciplinary decision to proceed with essential surgery. Incision begins, but delivery and all maternal and newborn outcomes remain unresolved.
