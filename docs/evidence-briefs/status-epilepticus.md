# Evidence brief: Status epilepticus

## Intended rehearsal

This intermediate adult emergency-department case rehearses recognizing generalized convulsive
status from seizure type, elapsed time beyond 5 minutes, and absent recovery; recording immediate
stabilization and point-of-care glucose in parallel; giving one fixed 4 mg IV lorazepam action; and
reassessing visible seizure activity, airway, ventilation, oxygenation, and escalation need.

## Evidence boundary

- The ILAE operational treatment threshold for generalized tonic-clonic status is 5 minutes. The
  vignette begins at 6 minutes 20 seconds with ongoing generalized bilateral convulsions and no
  recovery, so treatment should not wait for a longer historical definition.
- The American Epilepsy Society algorithm places basic stabilization, airway and breathing support,
  oxygen, monitoring, vascular access, and glucose assessment in the opening phase. From 5–20
  minutes, an adequately dosed benzodiazepine is first-line treatment.
- For an adult weighing 74 kg, the AES algorithm's 0.1 mg/kg IV lorazepam dose reaches its 4 mg
  maximum. This case accepts that single fixed action and authors cessation of the visible seizure
  signal. It does not model pharmacokinetics, physical delivery, or individual efficacy.
- If generalized convulsions persist after an appropriately dosed benzodiazepine, the 2024 ACEP
  policy recommends second-line treatment and finds fosphenytoin, levetiracetam, or valproate may
  be used with similar efficacy. This case names that escalation boundary but does not select or
  simulate one of those agents.

## Explicit exclusions

Screen controls do not assess injury protection, restraint avoidance, positioning, suction, oxygen
delivery, monitoring, vascular access, glucose acquisition, medication preparation or delivery,
airway skill, neurologic examination, EEG interpretation, repeat or alternate benzodiazepine,
second-line or refractory therapy, causal diagnosis or treatment, recurrence, team performance,
disposition, prognosis, or outcome.
