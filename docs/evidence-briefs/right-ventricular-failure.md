# Evidence brief: right ventricular failure

## Intended rehearsal

This advanced ICU case rehearses recognizing decompensated right-ventricular (RV) failure from
simultaneous congestion and underperfusion, reading a fixed pressure-loaded RV pattern, recording
individualized physiological support, keeping reversible triggers open, and reassessing trajectory.

## Evidence boundary

- The AHA right-sided-heart-failure statement describes organ injury from both venous congestion
  and underperfusion and emphasizes integrating cause, RV function, hemodynamics, and trajectory.
- ESC/ERS pulmonary-hypertension guidance frames advanced RV failure as specialist care. The case
  therefore records intent around systemic perfusion, oxygenation, acid-base balance, rhythm,
  preload, RV contractility, and pulmonary afterload without selecting or delivering therapy.
- ACC 2025 and ESICM 2025 support repeated perfusion, echo, phenotype, and hemodynamic assessment
  in shock. The authored CVP, wedge pressure, and cardiac index are prompts, not universal cutoffs.
- Preload is individualized. The case teaches neither automatic fluid loading nor automatic
  decongestion, and it makes no universal claim about an agent, target, catheter, or device.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not examine, acquire, calculate, diagnose, interpret ECG, laboratory, echo,
catheter, or imaging data, change oxygen or ventilation, deliver fluid, diuresis, or drugs, select
access or dose, perform a procedure, place mechanical support, transfer, determine disposition,
predict outcome, or assess pulmonary-hypertension, cardiac, critical-care, imaging, catheter,
prescribing, procedural, or device competence.
