# Acute pulmonary edema evidence brief

## What this slice teaches

One fixed hypertensive acute-heart-failure vignette asks the learner to recognize pulmonary edema
from the whole respiratory and hemodynamic pattern, check immediate mimics and precipitants, support
gas exchange with early noninvasive positive pressure, record decongestive and vasodilator intent,
and reassess breathing, oxygenation, pressure, and perfusion together.

## Current source floor

The 2021 ESC heart-failure guideline, unchanged for this acute pathway by the 2023 focused update,
recommends oxygen for SpO2 below 90% or PaO2 below 60 mmHg, early noninvasive positive-pressure
ventilation for respiratory distress with respiratory rate above 25/min and SpO2 below 90%, IV loop
diuretics for acute heart failure with fluid overload, and consideration of IV vasodilators when
systolic pressure is above 110 mmHg. The ESC acute-cardiovascular-care statement describes acute
pulmonary edema with respiratory rate above 25/min, SpO2 at or below 90%, and increased work of
breathing. A 2025 ESC Heart Failure Association statement retains IV vasodilators as initial therapy
when systolic pressure is above 110 mmHg, preferably in acute pulmonary edema.

## Authored vignette and modeled behavior

- A fixed older-adult presentation supplies severe dyspnea, orthopnea, diffuse crackles, elevated
  jugular venous pressure, cool but perfused extremities, respiratory rate 32/min, SpO2 90%, and
  blood pressure 188/112 mmHg. Fixed ECG, chest-radiograph, and focused-ultrasound statements support
  congestion while immediate ACS, arrhythmia, infection, pulmonary embolism, and mechanical-cause
  checks remain explicit without proving etiology.
- The live respiratory and hemodynamic anchors are authored. Noninvasive-positive-pressure and
  titrated-oxygen intent changes the displayed support setting; vasodilator intent produces a
  bounded pressure response. Loop-diuretic pharmacology, urine output, and individual response are
  not simulated.
- Reassessment reveals improved work of breathing, respiratory rate 22/min, SpO2 96%, and blood
  pressure 146/86 mmHg without implying that congestion, precipitant investigation, monitoring, or
  disposition is complete.

## Deliberate exclusions

This is not a diagnostic system, examination or ultrasound trainer, NIV setup trainer, oxygen or
drug calculator, prescription, or complete acute-heart-failure pathway. It does not model ECG or
imaging acquisition, biomarkers, renal function, electrolytes, urine output, diuretic resistance,
drug delivery or titration, contraindications, ACS treatment, arrhythmia treatment, valvular or
mechanical emergencies, intubation, shock, admission, chronic therapy, local protocol, or outcome.
