# Obesity hypoventilation reassessment

## Intended rehearsal

This intermediate Respiratory Medicine lab rehearses connecting a quiet daytime clinic state with
fixed awake hypercapnia and attended sleep-disordered-breathing evidence, keeping alternative causes
open, and coordinating respectful respiratory, sleep, primary-care, cardiometabolic, and
weight-health ownership without choosing treatment.

## Evidence boundary

- The 2019 ATS guideline defines OHS through the combined presence of obesity, sleep-disordered
  breathing, and awake daytime hypercapnia after excluding other causes. No BMI, bicarbonate,
  saturation, PaCO₂, or apnea-hypopnea index is sufficient alone.
- ATS supports serum bicarbonate below 27 mmol/L mainly to help exclude OHS in a low-to-moderate
  suspicion context; a higher result prompts PaCO₂ measurement rather than establishing diagnosis.
  When suspicion is high, ATS favors measuring PaCO₂ directly. The lab supplies both results as
  fixed reports and exposes no screening calculator.
- NICE NG202 supports awake arterial or arterialized-capillary blood gas and respiratory polygraphy,
  with transcutaneous carbon-dioxide monitoring considered during sleep. Oximetry alone does not
  diagnose OHS. This lab supplies an attended report; the learner does not acquire, score, calculate,
  or interpret it.
- The AASM adult diagnostic-testing guideline recommends polysomnography rather than routine home
  testing when awake hypoventilation or sleep-related hypoventilation is suspected. The fixed sleep
  report belongs to qualified staff and remains evidence, not browser-tested competence.

## Explicit exclusions

The controls do not examine; measure or calculate BMI or AHI; acquire, perform, score, or interpret
bicarbonate, blood gas, oximetry, capnography, spirometry, imaging, polygraphy, polysomnography, or
another test; diagnose OHS, OSA, or a cause; select or deliver oxygen, CPAP, NIV, an interface, mode,
pressure, backup rate, medication, weight target, nutrition plan, bariatric procedure, or treatment;
counsel driving, perform a procedure, change body weight, determine disposition or prognosis, or
predict response or outcome. All findings are fixed authored reports.
