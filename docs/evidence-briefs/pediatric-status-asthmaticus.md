# Evidence brief: pediatric status asthmaticus after initial care

## Learning contract

This lab rehearses treatment-record reconciliation, whole-child recognition of persistent severe
asthma after verified first-hour care, early pediatric critical-care escalation, qualified-team
second-line intent, time-aware reassessment, and active-risk handoff. It is not a diagnosis,
peak-flow, score, medication, oxygen, inhaler, nebulizer, intravenous-access, airway, treatment, or
disposition algorithm.

## Evidence anchors

- The World Health Organization's 2026 consolidated childhood-asthma guidance supports inhaled
  bronchodilator, antimuscarinic, and systemic corticosteroid care for severe exacerbation, then
  monitored intravenous magnesium as the preferred second-line therapy when available after severe
  or first-line-nonresponsive disease. It advises against intravenous aminophylline when safer
  alternatives exist and reserves adrenaline for a severe allergic reaction.
- The 2026 Global Initiative for Asthma strategy emphasizes frequent whole-patient reassessment,
  early escalation for ongoing severe signs, and urgent respiratory or intensive-care help for
  drowsiness, confusion, or a quiet chest. No single oxygen saturation, peak flow, rate, or wheeze
  intensity establishes the whole trajectory.
- The Canadian Paediatric Society describes repeated assessment of the same whole-child parameters,
  early PICU consultation for insufficient response, and monitored intravenous magnesium after an
  incomplete initial response. Advanced-airway decisions belong to the most experienced team after
  optimized care.

## Authored fixture

The child, established asthma, personal-best and arrival peak-flow reports, first-hour care, severe
minute-60 nonresponse, qualified second-line intent, and minute-90 partial response are fixed. The
healthy-child engine profile is source-composed scaffolding, not a validated asthma or treatment
model. Partial improvement does not prove causality, durable recovery, discharge readiness,
prognosis, or outcome.
