# Prehospital and emergency medical services

Title: Prehospital and emergency medical services
Status: proposed
Evidence briefs: none

## Why it looks strong

Care is time-critical, monitored, and made of scene-to-handoff decisions, and pediatric
prehospital cases are rare enough that providers cannot rely on experience to stay proficient.

## Learner roles and setting

Paramedics and emergency medical technicians. Needs a new prehospital shell: limited monitoring,
a limited formulary, and a transport clock.
