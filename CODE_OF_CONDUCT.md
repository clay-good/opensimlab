# Community conduct

Open Sim Lab welcomes learners, educators, clinicians, engineers, designers, and reviewers. Be
specific, kind, and evidence-led. Critique the work, not the person. Make room for different
training systems, regions, professions, disabilities, and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, credential attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation, and sharing private clinical or learner information are not accepted. Clinical
seniority does not excuse disrespect; technical authority does not substitute for clinical review.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, pause participation, or ban repeated or severe
abuse. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository Security tab once public; while the
repository remains private, invited collaborators should use the same private channel. Do not put
sensitive conduct reports in an issue.

Enforcement decisions should minimize disclosure, record conflicts, give the affected person a
clear outcome, and separate community conduct from disagreements about evidence.
